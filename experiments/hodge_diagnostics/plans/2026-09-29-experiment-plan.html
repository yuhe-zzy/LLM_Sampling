Paper B validation plan

Two weeks of neural experiments on 2 × H100 that test the adaptation–stability frontier, the two history-aware stabilizers, and when either matters in practice. Window: Wed 30 Sep – Tue 13 Oct 2026.

Proposed Drafted 29 Sep 2026, revised the same day For Fan Yao, Yu He Source plans/2026-09-29-two-week-experiment-plan.md

No experiment in this plan has run. Run-time predictions come from the actual IPO and DPO operators in cyclic_history/population_calibration.py; the stabilizer table and phase diagram are a uniform-reference design illustration from hodge/population.py, recomputed per panel before preregistration. Every run time is an estimate that the first measured runs replace.

Why these experiments

A JMLR reviewer will ask whether iterative preference optimization of a real language model obeys the paper's formulas, or whether the results stay inside the finite-response model. The answer has to be quantitative: predictions registered before the runs, computed from the theory with no fitted parameters, and then checked on LoRA fine-tuning with the operators the training code actually implements.

Where we start

25 Sep · Hodge diagnostics

Curated data are negative controls

HelpSteer, UltraFeedback, MT-Bench and Arena panels sit inside the frontier, so they cannot show instability on their own.

26 Sep · Fan's review

Four constraints

  1. DPO predictions use the actual pairwise BT optimizer, not entrywise logit(P).
  2. The raw sampler 0.2 · Unif + 0.8 · softmax(sequence sum) stays; a relative-to-initial sampler would be a different protocol.
  3. The current κ arm is oracle-assisted feedback extrapolation, distinct from the two-sampler estimator.
  4. Beyond the frontier the claim is generic nonconvergence with an oscillation signature, not a periodic cycle.
28 Sep · Yu He's calibration

Actual operators and a staged micropilot

Actual IPO/BT fixed points, Jacobians and exact trajectories; a balanced soft four-cycle; a 10-arm micropilot on six selected panels, ready after approval. Bottleneck: only 8 of 500 existing panels have every initial raw probability ≥ 0.005.

Correction to the 25 Sep diagnostics: “the pilot is predicted to converge” holds for IPO only (radius 0.929 with a flat reference). The actual DPO operator predicts instability at the same point (radius 1.289), and the prepared ν = 0.45 and κ = 0.25 do not stabilize it (1.021 and 1.292).

Testbed: panel-restricted iterative PsiPO

The paper's finite-response model, run with a real language model: the calibrated experiments/cyclic_history protocol plus three additions.

Frozen environment
Qwen2.5-1.5B, sequence sums including EOS; raw sampler μ = 0.2 · Unif + 0.8 · softmax(sequence sum); full-pair soft-label mode; cached log-score reference (1−α) s_0 + (α−ν) s_t + ν s_{t−1}; optimizer reset each round; fixed-point residuals and per-prompt cyclic-mode amplitude and phase logged.
Phase 0 panels
For 256 training and 64 held-out prompts, sample 24 candidates per prompt from the base model (temperature 1, ≤ 192 new tokens), score them, and keep K = 4 whose initial raw panel probabilities are all ≥ 0.05, preferring near-equal lengths; roles by ascending length. This keeps the raw sampler and removes the 8-of-500 bottleneck. If fewer than 128 prompts qualify, lower the threshold toward 0.005 and report it.
Predictions
Per panel and objective, population_calibration.py gives the fixed point, realized gain, ordinary and history radii, and exact population trajectories used as shadow runs. Failed roots stay “unresolved”.
Relaxation h
h_t = ⟨Δx_t, T(x_t) − x_t⟩ / ‖T(x_t) − x_t‖². If h ≠ 1, the relaxed map x + h (T(x) − x) is reported beside the exact one.

Metrics, all computed by script: log-amplitude slope and rotation of the cyclic mode, preregistered oscillating / converged / concentrated labels, step norm, fixed-point residual, KL to the computed π*(α), rounds to 90% of the terminal log-odds, last-iterate exploitability, and held-out quality.

How the plan changed

  1. v0 · the 22 Sep roadmap grid (9 α × 3 oracles × 2 objectives × 5 seeds, 100 rounds): too expensive, and most cells are predicted stable.
  2. v1 · controlled cycles and exact pair enumeration, so E2 and E3 test the deterministic theorems.
  3. v2 · calibration and preregistration: relaxation h, shadow population runs, a timestamped prediction file.
  4. v3 · separation: stabilizer configurations whose radii sit at least 0.02 from 1; α = 0.99 dropped; counter-intuitive cells added.
  5. v4 · realism: persona mixture, orientation arm, curated control, gated E6, model tiers.
  6. v5 · reconciled with the 26 Sep review and the 28 Sep calibration (same day): kept the raw sampler and replaced the relative-to-initial sampler with constructed panels that have interior support; made the actual IPO and DPO operators the prediction source; put the prepared micropilot first with gate G0; renamed the κ and orientation arms; corrected the IPO-only “pilot is stable” statement; restricted gain collapse to u = 0.
