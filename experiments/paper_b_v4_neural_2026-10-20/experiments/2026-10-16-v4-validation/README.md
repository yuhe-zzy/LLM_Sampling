# Neural validation of the JMLR-B-v4 theory (started 2026-10-16)

Fan's instruction (2026-10-16): run IPO/DPO post-training on the VM, choose suitable data, and predict before running. Results that match the predictions go into v4. When a result does not match, diagnose the cause, change the conditions, and keep trying.

The experiment uses the project VM `psipo-h100-1` (one H100, $6.98/h), started on Fan's approval. No paid API is used. v4 is the working version.

## Design

- **Theory-matched panel PO** (`vm/panel_po.py`).
  - A real language model, Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct (full fine-tuning), trains on fixed panels of K = 4 short answers per prompt.
  - Each outer round has three parts: a geometric reference (current and initial log scores), the sampler μ = (1−λ)·uniform + λ·π_t, and the exact pairwise IPO or DPO loss with soft labels and pair weights μ_iμ_j.
  - The loss is optimized from a fresh Adam for 40 steps. The population map of one round is then the paper's MRS-ΨPO map.
- **Panels** (`vm/build_panels.py`, `data/panels_q05.json`).
  - 600 prompts were tried: 65 templated short-answer prompts, 100 NoveltyBench curated prompts and 435 NoveltyBench WildChat prompts, each with an instruction to answer in at most six words.
  - Each panel holds the four most probable distinct answers among 48 samples, kept only if every initial panel probability is ≥ 0.05. 130 panels survived.
  - Every answer is scored by three reward models: Skywork-Reward-Llama-3.1-8B-v0.2, Skywork-Reward-V2-Qwen3-1.7B and GRM-Llama3.2-3B.
- **Oracles.**
  - Skywork soft labels σ(Δr/3.168) (Bradley–Terry) and hard labels 1[Δr > 0];
  - the three-RM jury vote share, which leaves only 1 of 130 panels without a Condorcet winner;
  - a planted-cycle control on the same panels (`analysis/make_planted.py`).
- **Predictions before runs.** `analysis/population.py` runs the exact recursion on the same panels and oracles. `prereg/PREREG.md` records the hypotheses (H0–H6), the pass criteria and a timestamped log; the frozen numbers are in `prereg/predictions_batch*.json`.

## Status (2026-10-17)

- **Batches 1 and 2 (H0–H6): all predictions confirmed.** The scorecard is in `prereg/PREREG.md` and `results/eval_main.json`.
  - Same target at equal β/(1−α): pairwise KL ≤ 0.008. The B = 2 control is 0.21 away.
  - Plateaus at the predicted targets: within 0.005 nats of entropy.
  - Hard-label DPO collapses in one round, and its log-odds keep growing.
  - Full refresh concentrates on the Condorcet winner on 100% of panels.
  - Planted cycles oscillate exactly in the predicted configurations, at fixed β and at fixed target, with matching amplitudes.
- **Two measurement amendments, both logged and both calibrated only on batch-1 runs.**
  - The top-switch signature was replaced by a detrended amplitude, because near-ties flip the argmax.
  - The amplitude is measured on probabilities rather than logits, because the losing answers' logits are unconstrained by the sampled-pair loss.
- **Integrated into v4 Section 8 and pushed** (Overleaf `80533b0`). Figures were generated by `analysis/gen_figs.py` and the text by `analysis/write_section.py`.
- **Batch 3 / 3b** (sampled pairs, Bernoulli labels): S2 passed at n = 32. S1 and S3 failed or were inconclusive at n = 32 because sampling noise is amplified by the step. With n = 512 every criterion passes. The fluctuation ratio at a fixed target is 10.4, against the predicted 9.
